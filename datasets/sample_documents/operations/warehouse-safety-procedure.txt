WAREHOUSE SAFETY PROCEDURE

Applies to all staff and visitors in the Rotterdam distribution centre.

VEHICLE RULES

The speed limit for forklifts and pallet trucks inside the warehouse is 8 km/h. Pedestrians have
right of way in marked walkways. Operators must hold a valid forklift licence.

PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT

A high-visibility vest and safety shoes are mandatory in all storage areas. Hard hats are required
in the high-bay racking zone.

FIRE AND EVACUATION

When the alarm sounds, leave the building by the nearest marked exit and gather at assembly point
B in the north car park. Do not use lifts. The shift supervisor performs a head count and reports
to the fire marshal.

SPILLS

Report chemical spills to the shift supervisor immediately and keep everyone at least 10 metres
away until the spill team arrives.

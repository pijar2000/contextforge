Incident Response Process

This page describes how engineering responds to production incidents.

Severity levels

LevelDefinitionResponse target
SEV1Customer-wide outage or data loss15 minutes
SEV2Major feature degraded for many customers1 hour
SEV3Minor degradation with a workaroundNext business day

Roles

For every SEV1 and SEV2 the on-call engineer appoints an incident commander. The incident commander coordinates the response and makes the decisions; they do not debug. A communications lead posts updates to the status page every 30 minutes.

  • Incident commander: owns the timeline and decisions
  • Communications lead: customer and internal updates
  • Subject matter experts: investigate and fix

Postmortems

A blameless postmortem must be written within 5 business days of a SEV1 or SEV2 incident and reviewed in the weekly reliability meeting.

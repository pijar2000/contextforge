# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees whose role does not require on-site equipment may work remotely for up to 3 days per
week, agreed with their manager. Probationary employees work on site for their first 8 weeks.

## Core hours

Everyone must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 Central European Time on working days.

## Equipment

The company pays a one-time home office equipment stipend of €400. A laptop and monitor can be
borrowed from IT instead. Furniture claims above the stipend need director approval.

## Working abroad

Working from another country for more than 10 days per year needs approval from People
Operations because of tax and social-security rules.

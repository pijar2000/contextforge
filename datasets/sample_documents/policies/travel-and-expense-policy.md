# Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting expenses

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase. Receipts are required for every
item above €25.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance on business trips is €60. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Flights and hotels

Book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. Hotel rates above €180 per night need
approval in advance.

## Approvals

Any single expense above €1,000 must be approved by a director before it is incurred.

Information Security Policy

Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is required for all company systems. Password managers approved by IT may be used.

Devices

All laptops use full disk encryption and must install operating system updates within 7 days of release. Lost or stolen devices are reported to IT within 2 hours.

Phishing

Report suspicious emails to security@halvorsen.example within 1 hour of receiving them. Do not click links or open attachments in a message you suspect is phishing.

Data classification

  • Public
  • Internal
  • Confidential
  • Restricted

Restricted data may only be shared with named recipients.

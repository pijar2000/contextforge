# HX-200 Hydraulic Gripper Manual

## Overview

The HX-200 is a two-finger hydraulic gripper for collaborative robot arms. It is designed for
pick-and-place work in warehouses and light manufacturing.

## Specifications

- Maximum payload: 12 kg
- Operating temperature: -10 °C to 45 °C
- Ingress protection: IP65
- Gripping stroke: 85 mm
- Mounting bolts: M6, tightened to 8 N·m

## Installation

Mount the gripper on the ISO 9409-1-50-4-M6 flange of the robot arm. Connect the hydraulic
supply at 6.5 bar and bleed the line before the first operation. Never operate the gripper
without a load-rated guard when the payload exceeds 8 kg.

## Safety

Do not place hands between the fingers while the controller is powered. Use the emergency stop
to release hydraulic pressure before any maintenance.

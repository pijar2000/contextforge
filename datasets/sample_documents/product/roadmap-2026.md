# Product Roadmap 2026 (Confidential: Product and Administrators)

## HX-300 launch

The HX-300 gripper, with a 20 kg payload, launches in the third quarter of 2026. Pricing will be
set 15 percent above the HX-200.

## Market expansion

In the fourth quarter of 2026 the company enters the Japanese market through the distributor
Kanzaki Automation. A localised manual is required before the launch.

## Deprecations

The HX-100 reaches end of sale in December 2026. Spare parts remain available for five years.
